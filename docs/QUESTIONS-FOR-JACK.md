# Questions for Jack (The Clear Out Crew)

The new pages only say things the current homepage already says, plus NYC Sanitation rules.
Each answer below unlocks stronger copy or schema. None of it blocks launch except #1.

1. **BIC registration (important).** NYC's Business Integrity Commission registers or licenses
   haulers that remove construction & demolition debris and commercial waste. The site already
   advertises "construction debris removal" and "commercial waste". Does the company have a BIC
   registration/license number? If yes, we'll show it (big trust signal). If no, we should soften
   the construction-debris and commercial-waste wording before relaunch.
2. **Insurance.** Are you insured (general liability)? Can you provide a certificate of insurance
   (COI) to co-ops/condos? Manhattan buildings ask for this constantly. Right now the pages only
   tell customers to ask their building what it needs.
3. **Donation / recycling.** Do you donate or recycle usable items? Competitors (Junkluggers) lead
   with this. We said nothing because we couldn't confirm it.
4. **Same-day / next-day service, hours.** Any real availability promise? Hours of operation?
   (Would go in schema `openingHours` and copy.)
5. **Electronics, AC units, R600a fridges, mattresses with bed bugs.** Do you take them?
6. **Pricing.** Minimum charge, or a "starting at" price? "How much does it cost" is the most
   searched question in this category; a real number helps rankings and conversions.
7. **Google Business Profile.** The developer's README says it's set up and verified. Send the
   public Maps link so we can link the site and the profile together (schema `sameAs` + footer).
8. **Social profiles** (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Yelp, Thumbtack, Angi). Links for `sameAs`.
   There's a Thumbtack listing named "Clear Out Crew" (founded 2020, 3-person team). Is that you?
9. **Years in business / founding year, owner name.** For the About section and schema.
10. **More real job photos**, ideally with the borough noted (no addresses). Each borough page
    gets stronger with a real local before/after.
11. **Reviews.** Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews. Don't put review stars in schema
    (Google doesn't show them for a business's own reviews on its own site).
12. **Google Analytics.** The site has a placeholder for GA4 but no tag. Create a GA4 property (or
    give Buck access) so we can measure the new pages.
