How much does junk removal cost?

It depends on how much there is, what kind of items they are and how easy they are to get to. Send photos or call and we'll give you a free, no-obligation estimate before any work is scheduled.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Estimates are free and there's no obligation to book.

What areas do you serve?

Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, the Bronx and Staten Island, plus surrounding areas. Include your ZIP code when you request a quote.

How soon can you come?

It depends on the size of the job and our schedule that week. Call or text (917) 533-3220 to check availability, and mention if you're on a deadline.

Do you work with property managers and contractors?

Yes. We handle rental turnovers, vacant units, renovation debris, multi-unit projects and ongoing work, and we take subcontracting jobs from waste and hauling companies.

Is there anything you can't take?

Some materials, like paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste, need special handling. If you're unsure about an item, ask us before your job and we'll let you know.
