Most Manhattan jobs come down to the building. Many co-ops, condos and rental buildings require a reserved service elevator, limit move-outs to certain hours and ask vendors for paperwork like a certificate of insurance. Ask your super or management office what they need and put it in your quote request.

Walk-ups are their own job. Five flights with a dresser takes time, so tell us the floor when you send photos and the estimate will account for it.

Street parking is tight. If your building has a loading area or a better spot for the truck, let us know.

Curbside or a crew?

DSNY picks up large items at the curb, up to 6 per collection day, but someone still has to get them down to the street. When that's the hard part, send us photos.
