Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information The Clear Out Crew ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if you give one), the job location or ZIP code, a description of the job, your preferred date, how you'd like to be contacted, and any photos you upload.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. If we use website analytics (such as Google Analytics), it collects similar usage information using cookies.

How we use it

  • To review your job, prepare an estimate and contact you about it.
  • To schedule and complete the work you book with us.
  • To keep records of our jobs and communications.
  • To understand how people use our website and improve it.

We don't sell your personal information. We won't post photos you send us online or in our marketing without asking you first.

Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your quote and job. We don't send marketing texts without your consent, and we don't share your phone number with third parties for their marketing. Reply STOP to any text from us to stop receiving texts.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host and form provider, email provider and analytics provider, and only so they can provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website doesn't use advertising cookies. If analytics is enabled, it uses cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Spam protection

The quote form uses Google reCAPTCHA, provided through our website host, to block automated spam. reCAPTCHA collects information such as your IP address, browser and device details and how you interact with the page, and Google may set cookies to do this. Its use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. If you prefer not to use it, call or text (917) 533-3220 instead.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and photos only as long as we need them to respond to you, complete your job and keep normal business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information you've sent us. Email theclearoutcrew718@gmail.com or call (917) 533-3220 and we'll take care of it.

Security

This website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to your information to the people who need it. No website or email system is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as financial or ID details through the quote form.

Children

This website is meant for adults arranging junk removal. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
